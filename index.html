About Nala

A one-person studio, built to run a page like a small team would.

NALA– Digital Marketing Services is a newly registered digital marketing business based in Talon Dos, Las Piñas City. It's run by Romeo Credo Cuyacot, who builds automation tools for small businesses that don't have time to manage their own Facebook Page day to day.

The current focus is two systems: automatic content posting, and automatic chat replies — both built to connect directly through official APIs so they run reliably and stay within platform rules.
